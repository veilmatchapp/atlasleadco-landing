Privacy.

A short, plain description of what this site collects.

What you send

If you use a form on this site, Atlas receives what you type: your name, email, business name, website, and any message. Atlas uses it to reply to you and to prepare your Checkup or call.

Who handles it

Form submissions are sent through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form service, and delivered to Atlas by email. This site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs.

Selling your information

Atlas does not sell your information.

What the site does not use

This site has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Waitlists

If you join a waitlist, Atlas uses your email only to tell you when that service is available.

Your choices

You can ask Atlas to delete what you sent by writing to the email address on the contact page.

This page describes how the site works today. If that changes, this page changes.
