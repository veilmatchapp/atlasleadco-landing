# Palette B: deep ink

Status: chosen by the owner on 2026-11-13. This is the site palette.

Intent: a cooler, steadier ground for a business that now sells security. A very dark blue-slate, the logo's gold as the single warm accent, warm ivory text, and muted steel and teal as real secondary tones.

## The logo on this ground
White lettering on ink is 17.17:1, and the gold figure on ink is 7.16:1. The gold stands out more here than on walnut because it is the only warm color on the page.

## Banned-list review
- Near-black plus one gold accent and nothing else: this is the main risk, so it gets real secondary tones (steel roads and grid, a teal river, slate panels) and light cool-paper sections. Gold is limited to the logo, the button and the town markers.
- Closeness to the rejected old site: the old site was a near-black navy with a gold accent and a three.js globe. B shares the family. What differs is the lighter slate ground, the engraved-line globe, the steel and teal tones and the paper sections, but the owner should weigh this honestly.
- Dark blue is also the most common look for security companies, so it is steady but less distinctive than A.
- No pills, arrows, eyebrow labels, numbering, gradients or identical cards.
