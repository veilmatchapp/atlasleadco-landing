Get a free Atlas Checkup.

Atlas looks at what anyone can see about your business online and tells you what it finds. It takes a short request form.

What Atlas looks at

  • How fast your website loads on a phone
  • Whether your website uses SSL (the padlock)
  • Whether the website looks out of date
  • Whether your email domain publishes SPF and DMARC records
  • Whether visitors can book online or chat
  • The basics of your Google profile

What Atlas does not do

The Checkup uses only public information. Nothing is scanned, no logins are tried, and nothing touches your systems.
