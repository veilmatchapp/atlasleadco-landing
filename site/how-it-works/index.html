How it works

The same four steps for every service.

  1. Start with a Checkup or a call

    Atlas looks at what is public about your business, or talks through what you need.

  2. Agree the scope and a fixed price

    You see what is included before any work starts.

  3. Atlas does the work

    Set up, configured and written down, on the tools your office already uses.

  4. Handover and follow-up

    Your staff learn it. Ongoing plans, like a care plan or a monthly security lead, are optional.

Fixed price

Before any work starts, Atlas writes down what is included and the price. If the scope changes, you agree the change first.

Available now, or on the waitlist

Each service is labeled. Available now means Atlas can do it today. Join the waitlist means it is coming and cannot be ordered yet. Joining a waitlist is free and does not commit you to anything.
